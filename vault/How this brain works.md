# How this brain works

1. You tell the **Lead** what you want.
2. The Lead reshapes it into clear tasks, each with a kind (research, build, write...).
3. **JEV** (the dispatcher) sends each task to the right member by its rules: no AI needed, so it costs nothing.
4. Anything about money, keys or approvals stays with the Lead and needs your yes.
5. Reports come back in the chat; grade them in Tasks so the performance review stays honest.
